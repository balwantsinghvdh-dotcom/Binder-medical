Please take clear photos using a smartphone or digital camera of the actual **Binder Medical** shop in **Ludhiana, Punjab**, and save them into the `assets/images/` directory using the filenames below. The website will automatically display them as soon as they are placed there.

## 💡 Practical Photography Tips for the Shop Owner
1. **Lighting:** Clean daylight or bright fluorescent shop lighting works best. Avoid dark shadows or strong backlight.
2. **Cleanliness:** Ensure the front board and counter are clean and uncluttered before snapping the photo.
3. **Format:** Both `.jpg` and `.webp` formats are supported. WebP is recommended for faster loading and higher Lighthouse scores.
4. **Resolution:** Keep files under 500 KB each so the website loads instantly even on 3G/4G mobile networks in Ludhiana.
